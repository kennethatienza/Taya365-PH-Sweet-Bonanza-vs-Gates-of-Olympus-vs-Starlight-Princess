### The Shared Grid Hides Three Distinct Styles

All three games use a **six-reel, five-row screen** with **symbols paying anywhere** on the grid. This means **matching symbols need not form lines**, unlike the fixed lines in many older slots. The key feature is **tumbling wins**, where matched symbols clear and fresh ones fall into place.

**Sweet Bonanza** uses fruit and sweets, while **Gates of Olympus** uses gems, crowns, and a Greek god. The **Starlight Princess** game has bright gems and a princess, not sweets or the god Zeus. A new look may change **the feel of play**, but **it does not mean better win odds**.

### Free Spins Start With Different Bonus Rules

The first **Sweet Bonanza** uses **four or more Scatter symbols** to start its free-spin bonus round. The maker says **Sweet Bonanza starts with 10 free spins** once the right Scatter count lands. The other two **start with 15 free spins**, which is **five more** than Sweet Bonanza gives.

In **Gates of Olympus**, **four or more Scatters** start the **15-spin free round** for that game. During free spins, **multiplier symbols add to a running total** and **boost qualifying wins**. **The running boost is a game rule**, not a promise that any round will pay well.

**Starlight Princess** also needs **four or more Scatters** to start its round of **15 free spins**. The **15 free spins** match Gates, and **both games use falling symbols** that clear with each win. Still, **the same free spin count** does not mean the games share all their rules or tools.

**Gates of Olympus** has **boost symbols up to 500x** in both base spins and free games. **Starlight Princess** has **up to 500x boosts**, yet that high cap alone tells us very little. Look at **when each boost works** and **how wins add up**, not just the highest boost shown.

### A Price Comparison Must Use The Same Stake

**A high win boost cannot raise the chance of a win**, even in closely matched games. **Each game can show blank spins and mixed results**, whether the stake stays low or goes up. **A short test cannot prove which game pays more**, since each spin has its own result.

For a simple example, **twenty spins at ₱5 cost ₱100 in total stakes** before returns. The same **twenty spins at ₱10 require ₱200**, even when both games use the same controls. **These sums are examples**, not real **bet limits**, since each host may show a new range.

A **bonus buy button** may appear with some versions, hosts, or markets, but not with all. **Buying a bonus** can cost far more than one spin, and **no win is assured**. **Check the full buy price and rules** before you treat the paid tool as good value.
